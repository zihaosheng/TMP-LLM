A Case Study with WisDOT WisTMP System

    WisTMP · 2019–2025

  2. 5,048 Instruction–answer pairs

  3. 6 Open-source models

    Three families · 7B/8B & 14B

  4. 6 Target sections

    Operational content · Sections 4–9

METHOD

Learn from completed WisTMP documents, then generate section-specific content from a new project's foundational information.

  1. 01

    Build the corpus

    Convert, clean and structure historical TMP documents into question–answer pairs.

  2. 02

    Adapt the models

    Fine-tune LLaMA, Qwen and DeepSeek models using low-rank adaptation.

  3. 03

    Draft locally

    Use project context and a target-section query to generate operational TMP content.

DATASET CONSTRUCTION

Sections 1–3 supply the context. Each available section from 4–9 becomes a separate generation target, yielding up to six training pairs per TMP.

RESULTS

Fine-tuning improves all six models. In this study, moving from 7B/8B to 14B does not consistently improve generation quality.
